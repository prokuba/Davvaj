House rules.

Every dare is meant to make someone's day better, including the person on the other side of it.

  1. Weird, never creepy.If it would make you uncomfortable on the receiving end, skip the card.
  2. Brave, never pushy.A "no" ends the dare. Smile, say thanks and move on.
  3. Film the moment, not the person.Ask before anyone ends up online.
